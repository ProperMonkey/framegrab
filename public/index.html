Frequently asked questions

What video formats are supported?
MP4, MOV (including ProRes), MKV, AVI, MXF, WebM, M4V, MTS, and M2TS. Camera RAW formats like R3D and BRAW are not supported — transcode to ProRes or H.264 first.
What resolution do I get?
Stills come out at the exact pixel dimensions of your source video. Drop in 4K, you get 4K. Drop in 1080p, you get 1080p. No resampling, no resizing.
How many frames will I get?
Roughly six stills per second of footage. A 60-second clip returns about 300 frames. A 30-second clip returns about 150.
How long does processing take?
Most jobs finish in under a minute. Larger files (1GB+) or longer durations may take 2–3 minutes. You'll see a progress indicator — leave the tab open until it completes.
Is my footage stored or shared?
No. Your video is processed in memory and deleted from our servers the moment your download finishes. We never view, store, or analyze your content. Read our privacy policy for details.
What's the file size and length limit?
Up to 2GB per file and 5 minutes of footage. If your clip is longer, trim it down before uploading.
Do I need an account?
No accounts, no signups, no logins. Pay $1.99, upload, download. That's the entire flow.
What if processing fails or my download breaks?
If anything goes wrong on our end, your session is automatically restored so you can upload again at no charge. If something else goes wrong, email TechnicianFilms@gmail.com — we'll make it right.
Can I use the stills commercially?
Yes. You own your footage, you own the extracted stills. Use them anywhere — portfolio, social, client deliverables, festival submissions, prints.
What output format should I choose?
JPG for portfolios and social (best balance of quality and size). PNG if you need lossless or transparency support. WebP for web use where every kilobyte matters.
